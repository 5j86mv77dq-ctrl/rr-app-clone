Vision

vision.md

Relevant Radio App — North Star

One place where the Relevant Radio audience prays, listens, and watches — live first, on-demand always, personal where it matters.

Pillars
  • Live is the front door — one tap from open to live prayer, radio, or video.
  • On-demand is the library — everything re-watchable and re-listenable.
  • Prayer is scheduled life — reminders that meet the day, not interrupt it.
  • Accounts serve continuity — resume, favorites, downloads; never a wall.

Deliberately not: a social network, a podcast aggregator for third-party content, or a paywall.
