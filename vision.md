## What the app is

One place where the Relevant Radio audience prays, listens, and watches — live first,
on-demand always, personal where it matters.

## Pillars

1. **Live is the front door.** One tap from opening the app to live prayer, radio, or
   video.
2. **On-demand is the library.** Everything re-watchable and re-listenable.
3. **Prayer is scheduled life.** Reminders that meet the day, not interrupt it.
4. **Accounts serve continuity.** Resume, favorites, downloads — never a wall before
   first play.

## Deliberately not

- Not a social network.
- Not a third-party podcast aggregator.
- Not a paywall.
