# Michael — The Committed Catholic

*Relevant Radio audience persona — v2. Built from the 2024 Listener Survey (n=11,361 respondents, ~9,924 app users). Owner: Peter Rowntree, created 2026-02-22.*
*Shared context — data snapshot, summary matrix, lifecycle and design principles — lives in `Roadmap/audience-personas-v2.md`.*

> *"I listen while I commute. It's the only time I have to go deeper in my faith."*

**Segment:** ~35% of current app users · Ages 45–64, married, working
**Strategic role:** Growth engine. He is becoming Margaret — deepening, giving more, evangelizing. Accelerate that journey.

## Who He Is

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 45–64 (sweet spot: 45–54) |
| **Marital Status** | Married with children (3–5 most common) |
| **Education** | Bachelor's (39%), Master's (17%), Doctorate (4%) |
| **Income** | $100k–$250k (35%+) · highest earning segment |
| **Housing** | Homeowner |
| **Has a Will** | Mixed — becoming more common as he ages |
| **App Downloaded** | 90%+ |

## His Catholic Life
- Weekly Mass · Occasional Confession · Wants to pray more but struggles with time
- RR inspired him to: learn faith (82%), pray more (79%), be a better spouse (68%), share faith (70%), go to Confession more (57%)
- Heavy podcast and on-demand consumer — Patrick Madrid, Father Simon Says, The Faith Explained
- Also consumes: Hallow, Ascension Press (Bible in a Year, Fr. Mike Schmitz), Word on Fire, Pints with Aquinas, Formed, Matt Fradd, Charlie Kirk

## His App Behavior
- **Listens on-demand during commute** — not primarily live
- Peak times: **8am–10am** (morning drive), **4pm–6pm** (evening drive), **9pm–6am** (wind-down)
- Browses **series and shows by name** — knows what he wants
- Spends **20–45 min per session** on streaming or podcasts
- Would binge a Fulton Sheen or apologetics series if he found it
- Watches video content occasionally — 20–45 min sessions
- Uses app prayer features but not daily — more "as needed"
- Shares content with spouse, adult friends, and social media

## His Job-to-Be-Done
> *"When I have 30 minutes in the car, I want to pick up where I left off in a series so I can keep learning without losing my place."*

> *"When my wife asks me a hard question about the faith, I want to find the Patrick Madrid episode where he answered it so I can share it with her."*

## His Pain Points
- **Can't resume where he left off** across sessions — no "Continue Watching/Listening"
- **Discovery is broken** — doesn't know what content exists beyond the shows he already follows
- App feels built for radio listeners, not on-demand users
- Too much scrolling to find named shows
- Wants depth but gets breadth — no topic-based navigation

## What He Loves About RR
> *"I share several talks per month with my own three adult children. RR is awesome! So informative — it's my daily CCD."*

> *"Relevant Radio shares the truth in love. I appreciate the consistency and courage of all the broadcasting."*

> *"Honest, truthful answers to questions and advice given with compassion & love. No judgment. You guys walk the talk!"*

## What He Wants Changed
> *"More shows — instead of repeating in the evening, come up with new ones for practical living like Marriage Unhindered. Shows for young adults, teens."*
> *"Different content during the 5–6 PM commute."*
> *"The 'podcast' version of episodes should indicate whether you've already listened to a particular episode."*

## What He Wants Next
> *"Video content — that would be exciting… also very capital intensive and risky…"*
> *"Catholic audiobooks"*
> *"Reels on social media"*
> *"More podcasts on daily issues of Catholic life. Are Married Life discussions recorded?"*
> *"Shows for young adults and teens that address their questions."*

## His Competitive Landscape
**Hallow** (daily use), **Ascension Press** (Bible/Catechism in a Year, Fr. Mike Schmitz), **Word on Fire / Bishop Barron**, **Formed**, **Pints with Aquinas**, **Matt Fradd**, **Jen Fulwiler**, **Angel Studio**, **K-Love** (music)

## His Financial Profile
- **Highest earning segment** — $100k–$250k household income
- Currently a modest monthly donor but **has capacity to give significantly more**
- Giving barriers: college tuition, mortgage, growing family expenses
- **Value-driven**: gives when he sees spiritual ROI for his family and the mission
- Quotes: *"My husband to get a raise."* · *"My other two triplet freshmen get a full ride to college… and I'd need to have a job."* · *"I already contribute. I'd want to hear how RR uses donations frugally."*
- **Future planned giving donor** — will have a will within 10 years. Plant the seed now.

## Design Priority
**Continue Watching/Listening front and center.** Named series easy to find. Featured row surfaces content he didn't know existed. Shareable clips.

**App zone mapping:** Zone 3 (Continue Watching) = Michael's front door · Zone 4 (Series rows) = his depth

## Desired Behaviors
- Opens app 3–5x per week for on-demand content
- Builds a series habit (Continue Watching drives this)
- Discovers and starts new series via Featured row
- Shares episodes/clips with spouse, friends, and on social media
- Increases monthly donation as faith deepens
- Becomes a Parish Ambassador
