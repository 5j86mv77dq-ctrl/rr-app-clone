# Margaret — The Daily Faithful

*Relevant Radio audience persona — v2. Built from the 2024 Listener Survey (n=11,361 respondents, ~9,924 app users). Owner: Peter Rowntree, created 2026-02-22.*
*Shared context — data snapshot, summary matrix, lifecycle and design principles — lives in `Roadmap/audience-personas-v2.md`.*

> *"Relevant Radio is like having a spiritual director on in the background all day."*

**Segment:** ~50% of current app users · ~61% of listener base is 55+ and 65+
**Strategic role:** Revenue base. She funds the mission TODAY. Retain at all costs.

## Who She Is

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 65–74 (core), extends into 75+ |
| **Marital Status** | Married (59%), Widowed (20%+), Single/previously married |
| **Children** | 3–5 (most common), adult children and grandchildren |
| **Education** | Bachelor's (34%), Some College (34%), Master's (16%) |
| **Income** | $50k–$149k (52%) · 22% prefer not to say · many on fixed income |
| **Housing** | Homeowner (85%+) · mortgage likely paid or nearly paid |
| **Has a Will** | ~60% yes · **only 3% include RR** ← massive planned giving gap |
| **App Downloaded** | 85–90% · **15-20% of 75+ have NOT downloaded the app** |

## Her Catholic Life
- Daily Mass (in-person or via app) · Rosary daily · Confession monthly+
- RR inspired her to: pray more (72%), learn faith (69%), share faith (58%), attend adoration (40%)
- 24% report **feeling less lonely** because of RR — understated in data, dominant in qualitative
- Has listened for 10+ years · Discovered via scanning radio dial, bumper sticker, or family

## Her App Behavior
- Opens the app at the **same times every day** — morning, afternoon, evening/night
- Goes **directly to live stream or audio prayers** — doesn't browse
- Spends **20–45 min per session** on live broadcast, prayers, or video (Mass, Rosary)
- Peak times: **2pm–4pm** (32–45%), **9pm–6am** (38–44%), **8am–10am** (19–25%)
- Reads emails RR sends · Occasionally visits website · Limited social media use
- Outside RR: AM/FM Radio (46%), Broadcast TV/Cable (40%), Books/Magazines (41%)

## Her Job-to-Be-Done
> *"When I can't make it to Mass, I want to watch it on my phone so I can still feel connected to the Church."*

> *"When I wake up at night and feel alone, I want to turn on the Rosary so I feel God's presence."*

## Her Pain Points
- Too many taps to reach the live stream or her daily prayer
- App feels designed for a younger person — text too small, navigation unclear
- Technology itself is a barrier (especially 75+ sub-segment)
- AM radio reception poor in some markets; digital is her backup, not her preference

## What She Loves About RR
> *"It encourages me in my efforts to be a good Catholic, closer to God. I'm 91 now and I'm cramming for the finals."*

> *"Listening is like having a friend, teacher and spiritual director alternative. One day the sun explosion affected our radio frequency and we didn't get radio for part of the day. I was surprised how sad and lonely I felt."*

> *"Relevant Radio faithfully teaches the Catholic faith with kindness and understanding."*

## What She Wants Next
> *"Any information about the saints' lives."*
> *"Gregorian chants and prayer-filled music."*
> *"Liturgy of the Hours tutorial — a daily broadcast for 3rd Order Carmelites."*
> *"Live Adoration 8–9pm with Benediction."*
> Most common answer: *"I can't think of anything"* or *"Keep doing what you're doing."*

## Her Competitive Landscape
**EWTN** (TV — primary), Bishop Barron homilies, The Chosen, CNA, Opus Dei podcasts, YouTube Rosary videos

## Her Financial Profile
- **Primary revenue base** — many are monthly recurring donors ($25–$50/month)
- Fixed-income donors give **sacrificially** — this is meaningful money to them
- Barriers: Social Security limits, medical costs, supporting adult children/grandchildren
- **Planned giving is the #1 financial opportunity**: 60% have wills, only 3% include RR
- Pledge sensitivity: ~65% say "just right" but **heavy solicitation feels disrespectful** to people giving sacrificially
- Quotes: *"I am 79 years old on fixed income."* · *"I donate automatically $50 every month for years."* · *"More income!!"*

## Design Priority
**Zero friction to live streams and audio prayers.** She should never have to search. One tap to her daily content.

**App zone mapping:** Zone 1 (Daily Prayer) = Margaret's front door

## Desired Behaviors
- Opens app daily at consistent times (habit loop reinforcement)
- Watches live Mass and Rosary every week
- Donates monthly — she is your primary revenue base
- Includes RR in estate planning (the $XX million opportunity)

## ⚠️ Strategic Warning
**This segment is in natural decline.** ~19% of the listener base is 75+. Within 10 years, a significant portion of this segment — and their donations — will be gone. Maximize planned giving NOW while this segment is alive and loyal. The pipeline of Committed Catholics (Persona 2) must be built to replace them.
