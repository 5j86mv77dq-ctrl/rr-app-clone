# Katie — The Seeking Catholic

*Relevant Radio audience persona — v2. Built from the 2024 Listener Survey (n=11,361 respondents, ~9,924 app users). Owner: Peter Rowntree, created 2026-02-22.*
*Shared context — data snapshot, summary matrix, lifecycle and design principles — lives in `Roadmap/audience-personas-v2.md`.*

> *"I grew up Catholic but drifted. Something keeps pulling me back."*

**Segment:** ~15% of current app users · Ages 25–44 · Fastest growing but smallest
**Strategic role:** The future. Longest customer lifetime value. Highest digital fluency. But she needs an on-ramp, not a firehose.

## Who She Is

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 25–44 (sweet spot: 35–44) |
| **Marital Status** | Married with children (1–5), or newly married, or single |
| **Education** | Bachelor's (45%), Master's (17%) |
| **Income** | $50k–$149k (61%) — still building wealth |
| **Housing** | Mix of renters (25%) and homeowners (55%) |
| **Has a Will** | Majority do not |
| **App Downloaded** | 91% — highest app adoption of any segment |

**This persona combines two real sub-groups:**
1. **Young committed families** (already active, pray Rosary with kids, listen daily) — ~60% of this segment
2. **Returning/exploring Catholics** (drifted away, coming back via a cultural moment, friend, or crisis) — ~40% of this segment

The survey confirms: 8.9% of all respondents said RR helped them "come back to the Church." This maps to ~1,000 people in the dataset. They are real.

## Her Catholic Life
- Ranges from weekly Mass to irregular attendance — faith is in motion
- RR inspired her to: learn faith (85%), pray more (81%), **be a better spouse (78% — highest of any segment)**, share faith (74%), have more children (27% — only segment where this registers)
- **Feeling less lonely: 40%** — highest of any segment
- Drawn in by Family Rosary (gateway), a friend's recommendation, social media, or a cultural moment (SEEK, Pope Leo, NEC)
- Discovered via: bumper sticker (33%), parish promotion (20%), family (15%), friend (10%)

## Her App Behavior
- **Digital native** — streaming is primary channel, not AM/FM radio
- Peak times: **9pm–6am** (57% — after kids are in bed), **2pm–4pm** (56% — school pickup), **6am–8am** (37% — commute)
- Listens to podcasts and on-demand content heavily
- **Watches video more than older segments** — 62% consume on-demand video outside RR
- Uses audio prayers on the app — Rosary with kids is a key habit
- The "returning" sub-group enters via **Featured content** — a specific event, clip, or cultural moment
- Doesn't know where to go after first content experience — **needs a clear next step**

## Her Job-to-Be-Done
**Committed sub-group:**
> *"When my kids are in bed and the house is quiet, I want to pray the Rosary on the app so I can end my day with God."*

> *"When I'm driving to school pickup, I want to listen to something that helps me be a better Catholic mom without feeling like I'm failing."*

**Returning sub-group:**
> *"When I feel drawn back to my faith, I want to find something that meets me where I am — not something that assumes I already know everything."*

## Her Pain Points
- **Committed:** Nighttime prayer experience could be smoother · Wants kid-friendly content · Needs spousal faith resources
- **Returning:** App feels like it's for already-committed Catholics · Can't find where to start · No obvious "beginner" path · Featured content is buried or inconsistent

## What She Loves About RR
> *"It's unapologetically Catholic!"*

> *"Relevant Radio has become such a big part of my daily routine. I listen to the rosary and Merry Beggars programming with my kids. I listen to Patrick in my office every morning."*

> *"Good and holy influence for my mind and time so not distracted by the world."*

> *"Relevant Radio has impacted my life in a lot of ways. I remember my dad playing it in the car when I was young — Bishop Fulton Sheen, Fr. John Ricardo, Matthew Kelly. They've inspired me a lot."*

## What She Wants Next
> *"Video content… that would be exciting."*
> *"Contemporary Catholic music program."*
> *"Saint stories, living saints spotlight."*
> *"Content for young adults and teens that address their questions."*
> *"Foster parenting in a Catholic home/setting."*

## Her Competitive Landscape
**Hallow** (daily), **Ascension Press** (Fr. Mike Schmitz — massive overlap), **Word on Fire**, **Pints with Aquinas**, **Formed**, **Jen Fulwiler**, **Minno** (kids), **Focus on the Family**, **Gabi After Hours**, **Pray More Novenas**, Christian music stations

## Her Financial Profile
- **Income constrained** — student debt, single-income families, young mortgages, growing families
- Willing to give but needs to see clear spiritual ROI for her family
- **Value-driven, not guilt-driven** — heavy pledge drives are counterproductive with this segment
- Quote: *"Content would need to have a bigger impact on my family or me."*
- Long-term: she will become a Committed Catholic (Persona 2) donor within 10–15 years if retained

## Design Priority
**Dual priority:** For committed sub-group → optimized nighttime prayer experience, kid content, family Rosary. For returning sub-group → Featured row is her front door. First content experience must be excellent. Clear series progression.

**App zone mapping:** Zone 1 (Daily Prayer) = committed Katie's nighttime habit · Zone 2 (Featured) = returning Katie's front door · Zone 4 (Series) = her depth path

## Desired Behaviors
- **Committed:** Opens app nightly for Rosary/prayers · Plays Saints content with kids · Shares with spouse
- **Returning:** Enters via Featured row (SEEK, Pope Leo, seasonal) · Watches one full series start to finish · Starts using audio prayers once comfortable · Becomes habitual over 60–90 days
